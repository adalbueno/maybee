How it works

One board. Four columns. Every idea moves toward done.

The board is the whole method: catch the idea, choose it, work on it, finish it.

Maybe→ Next→ Now→ Done
Maybe

Catch every idea in one place.

Type it in the bar at the bottom and press Enter. It lands in Maybe, where nothing is a commitment yet.

  • Rate an idea in a few taps and the best ones rise to the top
  • Ideas you decide against go to Not, with the reason why
Next

Choose what comes next.

Pull a few ideas into Next. That’s your short list: what you will work on once there’s room.

  • Drag cards to put them in order
  • Move a card with one click, back or forward
Now

Always know the next step.

Every card in Now has one clear next step, so you always know what to do today. When it’s done, Maybee helps you pick the one after.

  • Only a couple of things in Now at a time
  • Open a card to see the whole note, its history and its settings
Done

Finish, and see it.

Mark done and the bee celebrates. Done keeps everything you finished, so on a slow week you can see how far you came.

The agent

Click Do the next step. An agent does the work with you.

Maybee works with the AI assistant on your computer. It reads the card, does the step and leaves the result on the card for you to look at.

⚡One click per step

Drafts, research, outlines, files: the agent works on the step on the card and tells you when it’s ready.

Your second brain

A second brain, powered by AI.

Maybee isn’t only a board. Next to your ideas it keeps what your project knows: who you serve, how you sound, what you decided and who you work with. Everything is searchable in the Vault.

  • Knowledge: your audience, your offer, your voice
  • Decisions: what you chose and why, so you don’t decide twice
  • People: clients, partners and what you know about them
  • Outputs: everything the agent made for you

The agent reads your knowledge before it writes anything, so a draft sounds like you and speaks to the right person. The more you add, the better it gets.

Private by design

Your ideas live on your computer. Not in our cloud.

There is no Maybee cloud and no Maybee account. Your second brain is a folder on your machine, and it stays yours.

💻Runs on your computer

Maybee is an app on your own machine. Your board and notes never go to a Maybee server, because there isn’t one.

📄Plain files you own

Every idea and note is a Markdown file in a folder you choose. Open it in any editor, back it up, sync it, or take it with you.

🔑Only your plan is checked

The one thing Maybee looks up online is whether you’re on How I Premium, on Skool. Your password and e-mail are not stored.

Questions

Good to know

How do I get Maybee?

Join How I on Premium or VIP. The install steps are inside the community. After you install it, sign in to Skool once: Maybee checks your plan and opens.

Where are my ideas stored?

On your computer, as plain text files in a folder you own. Maybee itself only talks to Skool, to check your How I plan. When the agent works on a card, your AI assistant reads it under its own terms.

What does the agent need?

An AI assistant running on your computer, such as Claude. Maybee sends it the card and the next step, and the assistant does the work in your folder.

Is it only for business ideas?

No. Anything you want to move from “maybe” to “done” fits: a side project, a course, content, a personal goal.

What happens if I leave Premium?

Maybee stops opening, but your ideas stay where they always were: in your files, readable with any text editor.
