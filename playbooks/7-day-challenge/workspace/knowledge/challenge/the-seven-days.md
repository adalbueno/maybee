# The seven days

A 7-day challenge is an arc: participants start at a point, take small steps in public, and end with proof and the next problem. Each day takes about 30 minutes: 20 to do one step and post it, and 10 to help at least one other participant.

The content of each day is yours. It comes from your transformation: where participants start, what they can show at the end, and what problem comes next. The table shows the **role** each day plays in the arc, with the standard version of each step. Change the steps to fit your challenge and keep the arc.

| Day | The role of the day | The daily post |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the starting point: the problem, situation or goal they begin from | A title that names what they did |
| 2 | the first immediate step: the smallest action they can take today | A title that names what they did |
| 3 | the first visible piece: something real, even if rough | A title that names what they did |
| 4 | an outside reaction: someone else looks at the work and says what they see | A title that names what they did |
| 5 | the adjustment: one concrete change, shown before and after | A title that names what they did |
| 6 | the result: the smallest version that proves the transformation | A title that names what they did |
| 7 | the proof and the next problem: day 1 next to day 7, and what comes next | A title that names what they did |

**Rules that make an arc work**
- Each day builds on the one before, and day 7 shows the difference between day 1 and today.
- Every post names what the participant did, in one short update. A fixed opening line helps people follow each other; choose yours in [Set the rules](../../ideas/Set%20the%20rules.md).
- Helping is part of the day: end each post with a line that invites people to support at least one participant.
- Missing a day is fine: continue where you left off.
- A smaller finished piece is a win. Nothing is called finished that is not.
