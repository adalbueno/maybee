# Define day 2: the first immediate step

**Previous:** [Define day 1: the starting point](Day%201%20%E2%80%94%20say%20where%20you%27re%20stuck.md) · **Challenge index:** [Start here](7-day%20challenge%20%E2%80%94%20start%20here.md) · **Next:** [Define day 3: the first visible piece](Day%203%20%E2%80%94%20show%20the%20first%20piece.md)

Day 2 turns the starting point into action. Participants decide the first thing they will do, small enough to start today.

Start from your transformation sentence ([Define the transformation](Define%20the%20transformation.md)). What happens on this day is whatever moves participants from your starting point to your finish point. The example at the end is only one possible challenge.

## Decide

1. What should participants decide on day 2: a first step, a scope, a choice between options?
2. What makes a step small enough? Set a limit (time, size, one deliverable) so nobody picks something that cannot be done this week.

A starting point without a first step stays a complaint. This is the day it becomes a plan.

## Write the day brief

Fill in these lines. They become the daily post you publish and the guide participants follow.

- **Participants:** [what they do today, in one sentence]
- **They post:** [what goes in the post]
- **Post title:** [a title that names what they did]
- **Time:** [about 20 minutes of work, 10 to help others]
- **How they help each other:** [what a helpful reply looks like today]

**Done when:** a participant could read the brief and do the day without asking you anything.

## Example

A challenge where people start a newsletter. Day 2 is the first immediate step: the smallest action they can take today.

> My first step: three questions
>
> I'm building a newsletter for freelancers. My main difficulty is choosing which topic to start with.
> 
> Now, my first step is to write down the three questions freelancers ask me most.
