# Support the participants

**Previous:** [Run the week](Run%20the%20week.md) · **Challenge index:** [Start here](7-day%20challenge%20%E2%80%94%20start%20here.md) · **Next:** [Close the challenge](Close%20the%20challenge.md)

Each day while the challenge runs, open the daily place, reply to the new posts and support at least one participant who has not had a reply yet. Do not post anything for them. Write what you replied to in the workspace notes.
