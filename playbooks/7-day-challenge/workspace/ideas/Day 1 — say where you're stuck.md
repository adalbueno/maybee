# Define day 1: the starting point

**Previous:** [Write the welcome post](Write%20the%20welcome%20post.md) · **Challenge index:** [Start here](7-day%20challenge%20%E2%80%94%20start%20here.md) · **Next:** [Define day 2: the first immediate step](Day%202%20%E2%80%94%20pick%20one%20small%20thing%20to%20finish.md)

Day 1 opens the arc. Participants start where your transformation sentence says they start, and make it visible in public. It tells the group what each person is moving through.

Start from your transformation sentence ([Define the transformation](Define%20the%20transformation.md)). What happens on this day is whatever moves participants from your starting point to your finish point. The example at the end is only one possible challenge.

## Decide

1. What should a participant say or show so the group understands where they start? It can be a problem they have, a situation they are in, or a goal they want.
2. What is the smallest version of that, so it fits in about 20 minutes?

The starting point comes from your transformation, not from a fixed prompt. A challenge that starts from a problem asks for the problem; one that starts from a goal asks for the goal.

## Write the day brief

Fill in these lines. They become the daily post you publish and the guide participants follow.

- **Participants:** [what they do today, in one sentence]
- **They post:** [what goes in the post]
- **Post title:** [a title that names what they did]
- **Time:** [about 20 minutes of work, 10 to help others]
- **How they help each other:** [what a helpful reply looks like today]

**Done when:** a participant could read the brief and do the day without asking you anything.

## Example

A challenge where people start a newsletter. Day 1 is the starting point: the problem, situation or goal they begin from.

> Starting my first newsletter issue
>
> I'm building a newsletter for freelancers. Right now I don't know which topic to start with, and I haven't written anything yet.
