# Run the week

**Previous:** [Choose the start date and announce it](Choose%20the%20start%20date%20and%20announce%20it.md) · **Challenge index:** [Start here](7-day%20challenge%20%E2%80%94%20start%20here.md) · **Next:** [Support the participants](Support%20the%20participants.md)

Each day of the challenge:

1. Publish that day's lesson in the daily place.
2. Reply to the comments on the previous day's posts. A short, specific reply is enough.
3. Follow the [Support the participants](Support%20the%20participants.md) routine for anyone who has not had a reply yet.
4. Write one line in your notes: what worked, what confused people. You will use it in the closing step.

If someone misses a day, tell them it is fine and point them to where they are in the week.

The routine [Support the participants](Support%20the%20participants.md) is here to remind you each day. Turn on auto-run only if you want the assistant to prepare the replies.

**Done when:** day 7 is published.
