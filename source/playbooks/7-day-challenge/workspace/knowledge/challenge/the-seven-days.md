# The seven days

One small thing, finished in a week, in public, with other people helping. Each day takes about 15 minutes: 10 to do one small step and post what was done, and 5 to help at least one other participant.

| Day | Participants... | The post is titled |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | say where they are stuck | "How I'm" + what they are building |
| 2 | pick one small thing to finish by day 6 | "How I'll" + the result |
| 3 | show the first piece of work, even if it is rough | "How I'm" + what they are working on |
| 4 | ask for honest feedback on the work, with specific questions | "How I'm" + making it better |
| 5 | use one piece of feedback and show before and after | "How I'm" + the change |
| 6 | finish it, or finish a smaller version | "How I'm finishing" + the result |
| 7 | look back: day 1 next to day 7, with numbers | "How I" + the result + "in 7 days" |

**Rules that make it work**
- Every post from day 2 starts with one context line: "I'm building [what] for [who]. My main difficulty is [the difficulty]." Then a line that starts with "Now," and says today's step.
- Helping is a P.S. at the end of every day: what helping gives you, then "open the daily category and support at least one member".
- Missing a day is fine: continue where you left off.
- A smaller finished piece is a win. Nothing is called finished that is not.
