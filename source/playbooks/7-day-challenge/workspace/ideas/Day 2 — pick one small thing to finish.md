# Day 2: pick one small thing to finish

Template for the day 2 lesson. Keep the structure; write the opening and the "why" in your own voice, with a real fact of yours where the text says so.

## Day 2: How I'll finish one small thing this week

Today is about choosing one result small enough to finish by day 7.

If you pick something small, it gets done, and the people following you know what to ask about. *(Add one fact of yours here, if you have one.)*

Many people start with the biggest version of their idea. It keeps moving to next week, and nothing gets finished.

**Do the work**

1. Reply to the comments on your day 1 post, and write down any idea in them.
2. List 3 results you could finish by day 6. Then make each one smaller. A result is small enough when you can say what "done" looks like in one sentence.
3. Pick the one that helps most with the difficulty you shared on day 1, and write what "done" means.
4. Write your first step: something you can do tomorrow in 15 minutes.

**Or use AI:** paste this in the AI coach chat:

```text

could finish by day 6 that help with my difficulty, then make each one smaller. Say which ideas came
from the comments and which are yours. Help me pick one, say what "done" means and choose a first
step that fits in 15 minutes. Then help me write a short post titled "How I'll" + the result.
```
