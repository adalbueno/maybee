# Decide where it runs

**Previous:** [Set the rules](Set%20the%20rules.md) · **Challenge index:** [Start here](7-day%20challenge%20%E2%80%94%20start%20here.md) · **Next:** [Write the welcome post](Write%20the%20welcome%20post.md)

You need two places where people can post and reply:

- **Daily:** where the seven posts go. One place for everyone makes the helping step easy.
- **Review:** where day 7 goes, so the results are easy to find and celebrate.

A category in a community, a channel in a chat group, a tag in a forum or another shared space all work. Create them now, and keep the two links in your notes: the day cards point at them.

**Done when:** both places exist and you have the two links.
