# Define day 6: the result

**Previous:** [Define day 5: the adjustment](Day%205%20%E2%80%94%20use%20the%20feedback.md) · **Challenge index:** [Start here](7-day%20challenge%20%E2%80%94%20start%20here.md) · **Next:** [Define day 7: the proof and the next problem](Day%207%20%E2%80%94%20look%20back%20at%20the%20week.md)

Day 6 is the finish line for the work, one day before the review. Define what finished means for your challenge, including the smaller version people can settle for.

Start from your transformation sentence ([Define the transformation](Define%20the%20transformation.md)). What happens on this day is whatever moves participants from your starting point to your finish point. The example at the end is only one possible challenge.

## Decide

1. What is the smallest version of the result that still proves the transformation?
2. What can be called finished, and what cannot? Set the bar so nobody calls something finished that is not.

A smaller finished piece beats a bigger unfinished one. Your transformation's finish point sets the bar.

## Write the day brief

Fill in these lines. They become the daily post you publish and the guide participants follow.

- **Participants:** [what they do today, in one sentence]
- **They post:** [what goes in the post]
- **Post title:** [a title that names what they did]
- **Time:** [about 20 minutes of work, 10 to help others]
- **How they help each other:** [what a helpful reply looks like today]

**Done when:** a participant could read the brief and do the day without asking you anything.

## Example

A challenge where people start a newsletter. Day 6 is the result: the smallest version that proves the transformation.

> My first newsletter issue is done
>
> I'm building a newsletter for freelancers. My main difficulty is choosing which topic to start with.
> 
> Now, I'm publishing issue one: a single tip with an example message.
