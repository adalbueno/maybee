# Day 6: finish it

Template for the day 6 lesson. Keep the structure; write the opening and the "why" in your own voice, with a real fact of yours where the text says so.

## Day 6: How I'm finishing it

Today, you'll decide what "finished" means for this week and get there.

Finishing gives you something real to show tomorrow, and new ideas can wait for the next round. *(Add one fact of yours here, if you have one.)*

The end is where most projects stall. New ideas show up, each one looks small, and together they push "finished" another week away.

**Do the work**

1. Compare where your work is with the result you chose on day 2.
2. Write what's done, the one thing left and what can wait.
3. If it's too big for today, make a smaller finished version, and say what changed.

**Or use AI:** paste this in the AI coach chat:

```text
Day 6. Ask me where my work is now. Compare it with the result I chose on day 2. Tell me what's
done, the one thing left and what can wait. If it's too big for today, suggest a smaller finished
version. Then write a post titled "How I'm finishing" + the result, with the context line, what's
ready and one question asking for a final check.
```

**Share it**

Post in the daily place, titled "How I'm finishing it". A fictional example:

> How I'm finishing my first newsletter issue
> 
> I'm building a newsletter for freelancers. My main difficulty is choosing which topic to start with.
> 
> Now, I'm finishing issue one. The opening and the follow-up message are done. Today I'm reading the steps once more. Tool comparisons wait for issue two.
> 
> Is anything essential missing?

Tomorrow, you'll look back at your whole week.
