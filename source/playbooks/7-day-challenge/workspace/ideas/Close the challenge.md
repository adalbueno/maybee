# Close the challenge

1. Count: how many people joined, how many finished day 7, posts, comments. Use real numbers only.
2. Post a thank-you with the numbers, and name the people whose help stood out.
3. Read your notes from [Run the week](Run%20the%20week.md): what to change next time.
4. Decide what comes next: a second round, a longer program, a free trial for new members, or nothing for now. Write the decision in the decisions folder.

**Done when:** the numbers are posted and the next step is written down.
