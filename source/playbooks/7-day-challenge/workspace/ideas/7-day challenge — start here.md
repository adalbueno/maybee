# 7-day challenge: start here

This playbook helps you run a **7-day challenge** for your community or audience. In a week, each participant moves one small thing forward in public, and people help each other along the way.

## What the challenge is
Seven days, about 15 minutes a day: say where you are stuck, pick one small thing, show it, ask for honest feedback, use it, finish it, and look back. The full day list is in [The seven days](../knowledge/challenge/the-seven-days.md).

## What you get at the end
- You (the host): a ready-to-run program, a week of conversations in your community, and people who know each other's work.
- Each participant: something finished, seven short posts that show how they got there, and people who know what they are building.

## What you need
- A place where people can post every day (a community category, a channel, a group) and a second one for the final review.
- About an hour for the setup, then about 15 minutes a day while it runs, to reply and cheer people on.
- Optional: an AI assistant for participants. The challenge also works by hand.

## How this board works
- **Now** holds this card. Nothing else belongs here until you start working on a step.
- **Next** holds every step, **in the order you do them**: decide who it is for, set the rules, choose where it runs, write the welcome, write the seven days, announce, run the week, close it. Take the top card, do it, move it to Done, and the next one is on top.
- The day cards are templates. Fill them with your own words and a real fact of yours; keep the structure.
