# 7-day challenge: start here

This playbook teaches you how to create and run a **7-day challenge** for the people you choose to invite. In a week, each participant moves one small thing forward in public, and the group helps each other along the way.

## What the challenge is
Seven days, about 30 minutes a day. Participants start at a point, take one small step a day in public, and end with proof and the next problem. What each day contains is yours to define from your transformation; the arc and its standard steps are in [The seven days](../knowledge/challenge/the-seven-days.md).

## What you get at the end
- You (the host): a ready-to-run program, a week of conversations among the participants, and a format you can reuse or adapt.
- Each participant: something finished, seven short posts that show how they got there, and people who know what they are building.

## What you need
- A daily place where participants can post and reply, plus a second place for the final review. This can be a community category, channel, group or forum.
- About an hour for the setup, then about 30 minutes a day while it runs, to reply and cheer people on.
- Optional: an AI assistant for participants. The challenge also works by hand.

## How this board works
- **Now** holds this card. Nothing else belongs here until you start working on a step.
- **Next** holds every step, **in the order you do them**: define the transformation, decide who it is for, set the rules, choose where it runs, write the welcome, define the seven days, announce, run the week, close it. Take the top card, do it, move it to Done, and the next one is on top.
- The day cards are not scripts to copy. Each one asks you to decide what participants do that day, from your transformation, and gives one example challenge.

**First step:** open [Define the transformation](Define%20the%20transformation.md). Each card has a Previous and Next link so you can follow the sequence without returning here.
