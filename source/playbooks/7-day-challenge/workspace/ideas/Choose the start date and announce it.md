# Choose the start date and announce it

**Previous:** [Define day 7: the proof and the next problem](Day%207%20%E2%80%94%20look%20back%20at%20the%20week.md) · **Challenge index:** [Start here](7-day%20challenge%20%E2%80%94%20start%20here.md) · **Next:** [Run the week](Run%20the%20week.md)

1. Pick the date for day 1. Day N goes out on the start date + N − 1.
2. Announce it where the people you want to invite already are: what it is, the result, how long it takes each day, and the start date. Link the [welcome post](Write%20the%20welcome%20post.md).
3. Invite people to say they are in. A visible list makes the first day livelier.

If you take part yourself, say so. It tells people the challenge is real.

**Done when:** the date is public and people can say they are in.
