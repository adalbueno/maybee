# Decide who the challenge is for

**Previous:** [Define the transformation](Define%20the%20transformation.md) · **Challenge index:** [Start here](7-day%20challenge%20%E2%80%94%20start%20here.md) · **Next:** [Set the rules](Set%20the%20rules.md)

Write two sentences in your notes:

1. **Who it is for:** name the people you want to invite and the kind of work or change they are ready to move forward.
2. **The result:** write what participants will have finished, shown or learned by the end of day 7.

Change both to fit your audience. A narrow group works better than "everyone".

**Done when:** you can say both sentences out loud without hedging.
