# Decide who the challenge is for

Write two sentences in your notes:

1. **Who it is for:** people who are building something (a product, a community, a service, a habit) and have one part they have not figured out yet.
2. **The result:** "In 7 days you will have one small thing finished, seven posts that show how you got there, and people who know what you are building."

Change both to fit your audience. A narrow group works better than "everyone".

**Done when:** you can say both sentences out loud without hedging.
