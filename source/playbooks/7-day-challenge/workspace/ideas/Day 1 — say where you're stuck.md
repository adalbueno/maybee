# Day 1: say where you're stuck

Template for the day 1 lesson. Keep the structure; write the opening and the "why" in your own voice, with a real fact of yours where the text says so.

## Day 1: How I'm taking my first step in public

Today, you'll make your difficulty clear, in public.

When you do that, people know what you're building. They can ask how it's going, share what worked for them and notice things you missed. You also get a reason to come back tomorrow. *(Add one fact of yours here, if you have one.)*

Most people keep it to themselves until it feels ready. The questions stay in their heads, and the people who could help never find out.

**Do the work**

1. Write one sentence about what you're building and who it's for.
2. Write where you are now: what's done and what isn't.
3. Name the one thing you're stuck on. Make it specific enough that someone could suggest a next step. "I need help growing" is hard to answer; "people join but don't introduce themselves" gives someone a place to start.

**Or use AI:** paste this in the AI coach chat:

```text
Day 1. Help me find what I'm really stuck on. Ask me, one at a time: what I'm building and for whom,
what I want it to become, where I am now and what's getting in the way. Push me until the difficulty
is specific. Then help me write a short post titled "How I'm" + what I'm building, ending with: "Any
ideas on HOW I can get past this?"
```

**Share it**

Post in the daily place, titled "How I'm taking my first step in public". A fictional example:

> How I'm starting a newsletter for freelancers
> 
> I'm building a newsletter to help freelancers spend less time on admin. I want it to become a source of income.
> 
> I have a list of topics but no first issue. I keep switching between ideas because I don't know which one is useful enough to start with.
> 
> Any ideas on HOW I can get past this?

Tomorrow, you'll use the replies to pick one small thing to finish this week.
