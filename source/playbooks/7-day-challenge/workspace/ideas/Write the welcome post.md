# Write the welcome post

**Previous:** [Decide where it runs](Decide%20where%20it%20runs.md) · **Challenge index:** [Start here](7-day%20challenge%20%E2%80%94%20start%20here.md) · **Next:** [Day 1: say where you're stuck](Day%201%20%E2%80%94%20say%20where%20you%27re%20stuck.md)

Write the post people read before day 1. Keep this order, in your own voice:

1. **What participants will do this week:** move one project forward, with other participants seeing it and helping, in about 30 minutes a day.
2. **Why it works:** when you share the part you have not figured out, people see what you are missing and suggest what you did not think of. A real fact of yours here makes it believable.
3. **What usually gets in the way:** people keep it to themselves until it feels ready, and the questions stay in their heads.
4. **The week at a glance:** the seven lines from [The seven days](../knowledge/challenge/the-seven-days.md).
5. **How it works:** the [rules](Set%20the%20rules.md), in a short list.
6. **Optional AI coach:** paste the [AI coach prompt](../knowledge/challenge/ai-coach-prompt.md) and say it is for those who want it.
7. **A link to day 1.** Use a title and wording that fit your challenge; the day cards are templates, not fixed posts.

**Done when:** the post is ready and day 1 is linked at the bottom.
