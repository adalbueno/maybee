# Persona

Fill this in from the point of view of the person, not yours. A section that is only a prompt and an empty bullet counts as not filled in yet.

## Who they are

_One sentence: who they are, what result they are after, and through what means._

-

## What a day looks like

_Work, routine, hobbies, and how they take in information (which networks, channels, formats)._

-

## Their ideal scenario

_What a great outcome looks like for them._

-

## Pains

_What frustrates them today, in their own words._

-

## What they already tried

_Solutions that did not work, and why._

-

## Common mistakes

_What they do wrong while trying to solve it themselves._

-

## Transformations

_The visible change they want._

-

## Urgencies

_Why they want it now (an event, a deadline, a season)._

-

## Fears

_What they are afraid will happen if they try._

-

## Dreams

_What they want in the long run, and the stretch version of it._

-

## Objections

_Why they hesitate to buy or commit._

-

## Behaviors

_What they search for, watch and follow._

-

## Decisive moments

_The events that make them act._

-

## What builds trust

_Proof, results and signals that make them believe you._

-

## Their words

_Phrases they use to describe the problem, copied as they said them._

-

## What you would say to them

_One message that shows you understand._
