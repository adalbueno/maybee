# Offer

What you give people, laid out as a **ladder**: a few products in steps, from an easy first purchase to a deeper one.

- [ladder.md](ladder.md): the steps, what role each plays, prices and the expected numbers.
- [product.md](product.md): the template for one product. Copy it, rename it after the product, and fill it in.

Start with two steps: one entry product that attracts people, and one that brings the profit. Add the others later.

Every product points to the pains and transformations in [audience/](../audience/README.md) instead of repeating them, so a change there reaches every product.
