# Offer ladder

The products in order, from the easiest first purchase to the deepest one. Each step has a role:

- **Attraction:** easy to say yes to. Paid ads send people here. It can break even.
- **Profit:** where the money is made. Most buyers come from the step below.

Typical shapes for each step (adjust to your project):

- **Ebook or playbook:** a short document with direct instructions, templates or checklists. A fast, tangible fix.
- **Short recorded course:** three to five short lessons on one small, concrete transformation.
- **Group mentoring:** weekly group sessions over a few weeks, guiding people through one specific transformation with shared support.
- **Individual mentoring:** one-to-one sessions over a few weeks, tailored to one specific problem.

## Daily math

_For each step, sales per day × price is the daily revenue. Subtract what you spend on ads and remarketing. Check that the attraction step pays for the ads, then that the steps above it add the profit._
