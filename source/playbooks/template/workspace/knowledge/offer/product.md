# Product

One file per product. A section that is only a prompt and an empty bullet counts as not filled in yet.

## Where it sits

_Its step in the [ladder](ladder.md) and its role (attraction or profit)._

-

## Promise

_The transformation in one short sentence, the specific problem it solves, and what makes it different from what already exists._

-

## Pains and transformations it covers

_Link to the pains and transformations in the [persona file](../audience/persona.md). Do not copy them._

-

## What you deliver

_Each module, lesson, step or practical benefit._

-

## Format and duration

_Live, recorded, document, community, one-to-one. How long it lasts. What the buyer gets right after paying._

-

## Lesson or session names

-

## Benefits and bonuses

_The three biggest benefits. Extra materials or bonuses that make it easier to say yes._

-

## Content style

_How the lessons or materials look and sound (see [brand](../brand/README.md))._

-

## Feedback points

_Where and how the buyer gets feedback._

-

## Price and guarantee

_Price, installments, whether it should feel like a bargain or a premium, the guarantee, and how you say it so it feels safe._

-

## Access and capacity

_How close the buyer is to you, how many places you offer, how to reach you, any exclusivity for buying now._

-

## Objections

_Why buy now and not later. The most common objection and how you answer it, including "no time" and "too expensive"._

-

## Final check

_If you were the buyer, what would you change? Would you recommend it to a friend? How confident are you in the delivery, from 1 to 10?_
