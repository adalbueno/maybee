# What's new in Maybee 0.1.28

**Move cards with a click, ask your assistant the right thing for each column, and let it use a browser.**

- **Buttons to move a card** on the board and at the top of the note: forward, back, Not now, Reopen.
- **Quick asks that follow the column:** explore in Maybe, find gaps and refine in Next, work on it in Now.
- **Start** on cards in Now: with a next step it does it, without one it picks the first step itself.
- **Routines are cards like the others,** with a Run button.
- **No Ask AI button, "You + agent" label or ticket number on the cards.**
- **About shows the goal and the focus** of the project.
- **Your assistant can use Maybee's browser** in a window of its own. Anything that goes out waits for your OK.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you.
