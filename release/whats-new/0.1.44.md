# What's new in Maybee 0.1.44

**Maybee opens in your own browser, and fewer windows appear.**

- **Maybee opens in your own browser.** With Chrome, Edge, Brave or Chromium it opens as an app window, with no tabs or address bar; with other browsers, in a normal tab. When Maybee is already open, no second one appears.
- **The Skool check works in the background.** Maybee checks your How I membership without opening anything. A Skool window appears only when you need to log in, and it closes by itself when the check is done.
- **The check runs again when you open Maybee.** If more than 10 minutes have passed since the last check, opening Maybee checks again, quietly. If you logged out of Skool or left the community, Maybee asks you to verify right away.
- **Browser tasks for your assistant.** When your assistant needs a browser for a task, it gets its own window, and that browser closes a couple of minutes after the work is done.
