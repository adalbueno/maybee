# What's new in Maybee 0.1.46

**The work shows on the card.**

- **See what was made, right in the note.** When your assistant makes something for a card, it saves it in `outputs/` and adds it to the note under **Work**, newest first. Images, videos, PDFs and pages show inside the note; other files appear as links with a few words on what they are.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you.
