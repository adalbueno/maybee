# What's new in Maybee 0.1.26

**A badge for what changed, workspaces that show up on their own, and titles you can edit.**

- **A badge on Activity** shows how many changes you have not seen. It clears when you read them.
- **New workspaces appear in the menu** without reloading the page.
- **The card title is the note's first heading.** Edit it in the note and the card follows.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you.
