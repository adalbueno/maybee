# What's new in Maybee 0.1.42

**The bee introduces itself, the tour is new, and routines have a place of their own.**

- **A first-run chat with the bee.** A new workspace opens with Maybee asking four short questions. If something is too vague it asks one question back, then it sets up your project and writes the tour for you.
- **A new tour.** *What is Maybee* says what it is in one minute, and *How Maybee works* waits in Next as the step after it, with screenshots and links that open the columns of the board while the card stays open.
- **Routines move to the right edge.** The Routine column hides there, like Not at the left, and shows a yellow number when something is ready to run. A routine card looks like any other card, and you can drag them to reorder, skip one for today, or send one to Not.
- **Make a card a routine.** Drop a card on Routine, or use the strip on the right of a card in Done. A routine you send back to a column becomes an ordinary card again.
- **Routines open on their settings.** A summary line, and a new **Result lands** choice: a routine can make cards in Maybe, Next or Now.
- **A calmer board.** The columns step from light to a little darker, each with a hint of its own colour, and a card no longer shows its tag at the bottom.
- **One window.** Maybee no longer opens a second window when a session starts. The logo takes you home, to Now, and outside links open in their own window.
- **The bee answers at once.** It turns yellow the moment you ask, and red only when your latest request failed.
- **Changes show the time.** The Changes tab shows when each change happened, and new lines in the changelog get their time automatically.
- **Details:** space between the items of a list, and the same card size on every page.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you.
