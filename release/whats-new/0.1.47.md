# What's new in Maybee 0.1.47

**An Inbox, and a Vault made of cards.**

- **Inbox (it was Activity).** What Maybee made or created for you, grouped by card and with the column each card is in. New things are on top; once you open them they move down to Seen and leave after 7 days. What waits for your OK is always first.
- **Every output is on a card.** When your assistant makes a file, it goes on a card under Work and shows under its reply in the chat. A set of pieces, like 20 posts, is one note per piece plus one list, so each piece can be found and changed on its own.
- **A calmer board.** Cards show how many files they have, with a NEW mark when something arrived since you last opened them.
- **The Vault as cards.** The open folder is one big card of folders and files; images and videos show as thumbnails. The folders on the way close into columns on the left, like the board. Search is at the bottom, like the board's chat, and finds folders too.
- **Fixes.** Confirming your membership no longer opens a second window, and switching workspace keeps everything pointing at the right one.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you.
