# What's new in Maybee 0.1.45

**Maybee comes with How I Premium, and the membership screen is new.**

- **Maybee comes with How I Premium.** The membership check now looks at your plan: Premium and VIP members open Maybee. On the free plan, Maybee shows an Upgrade to Premium button that opens the plans page.
- **A new membership screen.** It looks like the welcome screen, says what Maybee does in three lines and keeps its button in view in small windows.
- **Your board is already there.** While Maybee checks your membership, your board shows behind the screen, read-only.
- **Back from Skool, Maybee checks by itself.** After you join or upgrade, come back to the Maybee tab and it checks again, with no button to press.
- **The welcome comes back if you skipped it.** If you chose Skip for now and the workspace still has its starting text, Maybee offers the welcome again the next time it opens, up to three times.
