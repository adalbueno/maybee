# What's new in Maybee 0.1.29

**Add a workspace in one click, start it from a template that knows your audience, offer and brand, and let updates move files without breaking your links.**

- **Add workspace** in the workspace menu creates a new workspace from a blank template and opens its Workspace board. No assistant needed.
- **A template with real structure:** knowledge folders for your audience, your offer (a ladder of products) and your brand (voice and the elements that make you recognizable), and a decisions folder with one file per decision.
- **One Maybee skill for your assistant,** with guides for the board, for writing in your voice from those files, and for moving files safely.
- **Updates can move your files, only with your OK.** Your assistant shows what would move, you say yes, and it fixes every link and keeps a backup. This update proposes moving your voice note to `knowledge/brand/` and your decision register to `decisions/`.
- **Links stay correct.** When your assistant moves, renames or deletes a file, it checks that no link is left pointing at nothing.
- **The app reloads itself** after Maybee restarts, and the workspace menu says why when a workspace cannot be opened.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you, except answering yes when your assistant offers to move the two files.
