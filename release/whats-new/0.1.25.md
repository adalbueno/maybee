# What's new in Maybee 0.1.25

**Write an idea straight into Maybe, and arrange your cards by hand.**

- **A field at the bottom of the Maybe column.** Type an idea and press Enter: it becomes a card at the top of the column.
- **Drag cards into your own order** in Maybe, Next and Now. A card you move to another column lands at the top of it.
- **Sort by priority, once,** with the link under the Maybe title. You can still drag cards after.
- **One button less at the top.** Story / Everything is now "Project files" inside Activity. Those files are always in the Vault.
- **Maybee's own files stay out of sight.** Nothing from the `.maybee` folder shows in Activity, the Vault or search.
- **One dot instead of two buttons.** Green: listening. Grey: nobody is listening. Red: offline. Click it to see the requests.
- **About is the project's cover.** Name, a few numbers, shortcuts to the places and to the project's own files.
- **A clearer "Not listening".** Maybee says when the last session stopped and what to write in Claude to start it again.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you.
