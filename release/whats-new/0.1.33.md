# What's new in Maybee 0.1.33

**Put an image, a video, a page or a PDF inside a note, and see it right there.**

- **`![](file)` shows the file.** A line that holds only `![alt](path)` or `![[file]]` is drawn in the note: an image, a video, or a page or PDF in a frame. This works when you read the note and in the editor.
- **Editing shows the text.** Double click the note and the Markdown appears as you typed it, so you can change it. Esc, or leaving the note, draws the files again.
- **Pages run on their own.** A page in a frame can run its scripts but cannot reach Maybee or your notes.
- **Only on its own line.** An image in the middle of a sentence stays as text in the editor.
