# What's new in Maybee 0.1.24

**Ask AI is now a chat at the bottom of every note, and notes are always ready to edit.**

- **A chat that floats at the bottom of the note.** Write to your assistant about the note you are reading. The conversation about that note, with the answers, opens above it. Enter sends, Esc hides it.
- **Notes open ready to edit.** There is no Edit button: click and type. Maybee saves a moment after you stop typing, and when you switch notes or tabs.
- **Changes made elsewhere are taken in quietly.** If your assistant or the Settings tab changes the note while it is open, you keep what you typed. Only if the text itself changed on both sides, Maybee asks which version to keep.
- **Fewer windows when checking your membership.** A check that fails to start no longer opens a pile of windows.
