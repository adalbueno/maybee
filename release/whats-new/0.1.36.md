# What's new in Maybee 0.1.36

**Your assistant starts, stops and restarts Maybee with one command, and the bridge stays on.**

- **`/maybee` does the right thing.** Alone, it makes everything run: the app and the bridge, leaving alone what already runs. With a word it is `start`, `stop`, `restart`, `listen`, `status` or `update`.
- **The bridge is started with the app.** It used to be a second step an assistant could forget. Now the install is not finished until the app says "Listening" with the assistant's name.
- **Codex: no time limit.** The bridge keeps answering until you stop it.
- **Codex: no more silent failures.** A bridge started inside Codex's sandbox could say "Listening" and then fail every request, because the sandbox does not let Codex write its own folder. It now checks at the start and says what to do: run it outside the sandbox, with your approval.
- **Only one bridge at a time** answers for a workspace.
