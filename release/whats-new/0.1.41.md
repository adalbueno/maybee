# What's new in Maybee 0.1.41

**Cards move with one click, Not can be cleaned up, and Maybee opens in a single window.**

- **Move a card from its sides.** Point at the left or right half of a card and a strip slides out, in the colour of the column the card goes to: one step back on the left, one step forward on the right. Dragging still works, and the drag now shows a clean copy of the card.
- **Delete a card in Not.** In Not, the left strip deletes the card (click twice to confirm) and the right one sends it back to Maybee. A deleted card is kept in `_to_delete/`, so a mistake can be undone.
- **Priority is a small signal.** The priority sits in the corner of the card as 1 to 5 bars instead of a row of its own, and Maybee shows every card, not only the first 15.
- **The bee turns red** when the last request you sent could not be answered.
- **One window.** Maybee no longer opens a second window when a session starts or when you ask to open it again; asking to open it after you closed it opens it again.
- **The 7-day challenge playbook** now guides you to define the transformation first, then shape each day, then launch.
