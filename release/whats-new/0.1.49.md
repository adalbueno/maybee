# What's new in Maybee 0.1.49

**A Brain you can ask, and an Inbox like mail.**

- **The Vault is now the Brain.** Your project, its state and its dashboard sit on top; the folders below.
- **Ask the Brain.** Type to search. Press Enter to ask a question: Maybee answers from your files, with links. You can also tell it to change your goal, audience or voice, and it updates the right file.
- **Move, rename and delete files in the Brain.** Drag a file onto a folder, or use the ⋯ menu. Every link to it is fixed. A deleted file goes to the trash, with Undo. Project, State, Dashboard and the logs stay where Maybee needs them.
- **About is gone.** What it showed lives in the Brain, the Inbox and the board.
- **The Inbox has two boxes.** **Needs you**: what waits for your OK. **For you**: what Maybee made, one line per card. Unread lines are bold, and each card's column shows as a tag.
- **A chat in the Inbox.** Answer what needs you or approve what arrived without leaving it.
- **Every place slides like the board.** The Inbox and the Brain's folders open and close as columns.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you.
