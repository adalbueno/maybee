# What's new in Maybee 0.1.30

**Move any note, find your audience, offer and brand in About, and let updates tidy your files on their own.**

- **A note with no frontmatter can be moved.** Maybee adds the frontmatter itself the first time you move the card or change its settings. It used to refuse.
- **About lists your audience, offer, brand and decisions,** and shows how much of each file is filled in ("3 of 16 filled").
- **The Owner group is gone from About,** to leave room for the groups above.
- **Older workspaces can get the new files.** If a workspace lacks them, About offers to add them. It only adds what is missing and never touches a file that exists.
- **Updates move your files by themselves.** When Maybee starts after an update, it moves the files the update asks for, fixes the links to them and keeps a backup in `.maybee/backup/`. If an old and a new copy of a file both exist and differ, it leaves both and tells your assistant. (The 0.1.29 notes said this needed your OK. It does not.)

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you.
