# What's new in Maybee 0.1.51

**Show in folder, and a tour that starts from the method.**

- **Show in folder.** A folder button on every card, and an item in the Brain's ⋯ menu, open your file manager on the file, selected (Show in Finder on a Mac, Show in Explorer on Windows). On a folder, it opens the folder.
- **A clearer menu in the Brain.** The ⋯ menu no longer gets cut by its card, and the files Maybee keeps in place (Project, State, the logs) have it too, with just Show in folder.
- **A new tour for new projects.** *How Maybee works* now starts from the method: every idea starts in Maybe with no promise attached, then gets one decision (no goes to Not with its reason, yes becomes a plan in Next, not yet stays in Maybe). The cards and pictures show the Inbox, the Brain and the Routine column at the right edge.
- **A daily check-in instead of the post routine.** New projects start with a routine that reads the board and suggests the one thing worth doing today. It only suggests; it never moves a card.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you.
