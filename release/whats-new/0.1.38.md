# What's new in Maybee 0.1.38

**Playbooks: a ready-made set of cards you can start from or add to a workspace you already have.**

- **What a playbook is.** A folder shaped like a workspace (ideas, routines, notes, decisions) with a small `playbook.json`. It has no project, state or dashboard file, because it adds to your workspace instead of replacing anything.
- **Import a .zip.** In the workspace menu, choose **Import playbook** and pick a `.zip`. Files you already have are left exactly as they are, and a message at the bottom tells you how many files were added. Routines come in with auto-run off, so nothing runs until you turn it on.
- **Start a workspace from one.** **Add workspace** shows a list of playbooks when there are any, with a blank workspace as the first choice.
- **The first playbook: 7-day challenge.** Run a 7-day challenge for your community or audience. **Now** holds one card that explains the playbook, and **Next** holds every step in the order you do it: who it is for, the rules, where it runs, the welcome post, the seven days, the announcement, the week itself and the closing.
- **Messages at the bottom.** Results now appear as a short message at the bottom of the page: green when it worked, red when it did not.
- **New ideas have no code.** A new card no longer gets an `I12`-style id. A card is its file. Your existing ids stay as they are.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you.
