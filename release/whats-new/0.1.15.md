# What's new in Maybee 0.1.15

**Maybee now checks your How I membership by itself, and opens in a window of its own.**

- **You no longer need your assistant for the check.** Maybee opens its own browser window (it uses Chrome, Edge, Brave or Chromium, whichever you already have). If you are signed in to Skool, it confirms you in a few seconds. If you are not, a Skool window opens so you can log in, and the check finishes right after.
- **Maybee keeps only what it needs** to confirm access: your user, the community, your role and the date you were approved. It never stores your cookies or password.
- **A window of its own.** Maybee now opens in its own window, not as a tab in your everyday browser.
- **The demo mode is gone.** There is no way to skip the membership check anymore.

**Done when:** you have read this and moved the card to Done. Nothing is required from you: this works without any setup.
