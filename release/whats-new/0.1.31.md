# What's new in Maybee 0.1.31

**Follow the links in your notes, edit tables right where they are, and find your files faster in About.**

- **Notes open for reading.** A click on a link follows it. A double click turns editing on, with a yellow border, and Esc turns it off. Everything else looks the same, and saving is automatic as before.
- **Tables look like tables.** In a note you see a real table. While editing, you type straight into its cells: Tab and Enter move around, Tab in the last cell adds a row, and "+ Row", "+ Column" and "Edit as text" sit under it.
- **About is easier to read.** Each group starts with its Overview, files have readable names (Design elements, Project), and a small ring beside a name shows what is still empty: dashed when nothing is filled in, an arc while it is partly filled, solid when it is complete. Under a name you now see only its description.
- **New workspaces start with descriptions** on the audience, offer and brand files, and a decision is described by its frontmatter.
