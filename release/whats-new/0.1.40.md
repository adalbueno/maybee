# What's new in Maybee 0.1.40

**The bee tells you what the assistant is doing, and board columns slide open.**

- **The bee is the status.** In the top bar and on the chat button at the bottom, the bee is **grey** when no session is listening, **green** when one is, and **yellow, swaying**, while it works on a request. When the work ends it gives one pulse and goes back to green.
- **Board columns slide open.** Opening a column now slides its content in, quickly, instead of popping in.
- **The chat says Maybee.** The chat now reads "Ask Maybee about this note", and replies are signed Maybee.
