# What's new in Maybee

The newest version is at the top. Maybee keeps this file up to date when you update, so it always shows the full history. It is not part of your notes: edit your own files, not this one.

## 0.1.51 · 26 Nov 2026

**Show in folder, and a tour that starts from the method.**

- **Show in folder**: a folder button on every card and an item in the Brain's ⋯ menu open your file manager on the file (Show in Finder on a Mac, Show in Explorer on Windows).
- **The Brain's ⋯ menu** shows whole instead of being cut by its card; fixed files (Project, State, logs) have it with just Show in folder.
- **A new tour for new projects**: every idea starts in Maybe and gets one decision (no, yes or not yet); the cards and pictures show the Inbox, the Brain and the Routine column at the right edge.
- **A daily check-in** replaces the How I post routine in new projects: it reads the board and suggests the one thing worth doing today.

## 0.1.50 · 25 Nov 2026

**Attach files in every chat.**

- **Attach files** in the board's, the Inbox's, the Brain's and every card's chat: the paperclip, drop a file, or paste a screenshot; an icon shows each file's kind, and Maybee reads them first.
- **Files stay in the project**, in `attachments/` (shown in the Brain); the same file attached twice is kept once.
- **On the board**, a file attached in the bar goes on the new card; a file sent alone names the card.
- **One chat in sight**: with a card open, the board's bar shows while your mouse is over the board, the card's chat while it is over the card.
- **A card can fill the window**, with the button next to ✕.

## 0.1.49 · 23 Nov 2026

**A Brain you can ask, and an Inbox like mail.**

- **Brain** replaces the Vault: the project, its state and its dashboard on top, the folders below; ask it questions or tell it what to change in the chat.
- **Move, rename and delete** files in the Brain: drag onto a folder or use ⋯; every link follows; deleted files go to the trash, with Undo.
- **About is gone**: its parts live in the Brain, the Inbox and the board.
- **Inbox**: two boxes, Needs you and For you (one line per card, unread in bold, the card's column as a tag), and a chat to answer and approve.
- **Every place slides like the board**: the Inbox and the Brain's folders open and close as columns.

## 0.1.48 · 23 Nov 2026

**A tidier Vault.**

- **The first column** shows a smaller bee and your file count; the workspace's name shows on hover and as the title at the top level.

## 0.1.47 · 23 Nov 2026

**An Inbox, and a Vault made of cards.**

- **Inbox** replaces Activity: what Maybee made or created for you, by card, new on top, plus what waits for your OK.
- **Every output is on a card**, under Work and under its reply in the chat; a set of pieces is one note per piece.
- **Board cards** show a file count with a NEW mark.
- **Vault**: folders and files as cards, the path as columns like the board, search at the bottom.
- **Fixes**: no second window after confirming membership; switching workspace keeps scripts on the right one.

## 0.1.46 · 21 Nov 2026

**The work shows on the card.**

- **What your assistant makes for a card** goes in the note under Work: images, videos, PDFs and pages show inside the note, other files as links.

## 0.1.45 · 21 Nov 2026

**Maybee comes with How I Premium, and the membership screen is new.**

- **How I Premium:** Premium and VIP members open Maybee; the free plan gets an Upgrade to Premium button.
- **A new membership screen,** with your board already showing behind it, read-only.
- **The welcome comes back** the next time Maybee opens if you skipped it and the workspace still has its starting text, up to three times.

## 0.1.44 · 20 Nov 2026

**Maybee opens in your own browser, and fewer windows appear.**

- **In your own browser:** an app window with Chrome, Edge, Brave or Chromium, a normal tab otherwise, and never a second one.
- **The Skool check works in the background.** A window appears only when you need to log in, and closes by itself.
- **Opening Maybee checks again** when the last check is more than 10 minutes old.

## 0.1.43 · 20 Nov 2026

**One clear AI action per card, quick capture from anywhere on the board, and a bee that celebrates finished work.**

- **A new welcome:** a welcome screen, your name and one open question, and how Maybee works while your assistant reads it. With no assistant connected, the board opens directly.
- **One AI action per column:** Explore this idea, Get it ready to start, Do the next step, What comes next?. It sits on the card's chat bar and turns into Send while you type.
- **A capture bar under the board.** Write an idea, press Enter, and the new card shows up highlighted in Maybe.
- **The bee celebrates** when finished work lands in Done. The list behind it shows one short row per card and opens the card with its chat.
- **Fewer windows on the membership check:** only the button opens Skool.
- **Maybee repairs itself** when something fails, and prepares a filled-in GitHub report for what it cannot fix. Windows fixes for the listener and Codex from the Microsoft Store.

- **A new tour.** *What is Maybee*, then *How Maybee works* in Next, with screenshots and links that open the columns.

## 0.1.41 · 17 Nov 2026

**Cards move with one click, Not can be cleaned up, and Maybee opens in a single window.**

- **Move a card from its sides.** A strip slides out on the left or right half of a card, in the colour of the column it goes to. Dragging still works.
- **Delete a card in Not.** The left strip deletes it (click twice); the card is kept in `_to_delete/`. The right one sends it back to Maybee.
- **Priority is a small signal.** 1 to 5 bars in the corner of the card, and every card is shown, not only the first 15.
- **The bee turns red** when the last request could not be answered.
- **One window.** No second window when a session starts or when you ask to open Maybee again.
- **The 7-day challenge playbook** starts with defining the transformation.

## 0.1.40 · 16 Nov 2026

**The bee tells you what the assistant is doing, and board columns slide open.**

- **The bee is the status.** In the top bar and on the chat button, it is grey when no session is listening, green when one is, and yellow and swaying while it works. When the work ends it pulses once and goes back to green.
- **Board columns slide open.** A column's content slides in quickly when you open it.
- **The chat says Maybee.** "Ask Maybee about this note", and replies are signed Maybee.

## 0.1.39 · 16 Nov 2026

**Playbooks are zip files you import. A new workspace always starts from the default template.**

- **A correction to 0.1.38:** **Add workspace** does not offer a list of playbooks. A new workspace always starts from the default template.
- **Playbooks are separate from Maybee.** Each one is a `.zip` you import with **Import playbook**; nothing already in your workspace is overwritten, and routines come in with auto-run off.
- **The 7-day challenge is a playbook.** Download `playbook-7-day-challenge.zip` from this release's page on GitHub and import it.

## 0.1.38 · 16 Nov 2026

**Playbooks: a ready-made set of cards you can start from or add to a workspace you already have.**

- **Import a .zip.** In the workspace menu, **Import playbook** adds a playbook's files to the workspace you have open. Files you already have are left as they are, and routines come in with auto-run off.
- **Start a workspace from one.** **Add workspace** lists the playbooks, with a blank workspace first.
- **The first playbook: 7-day challenge.** One card in Now explains it; Next has every step in order.
- **Messages at the bottom.** Results show as a short message: green when it worked, red when it did not.
- **A new logo.** The yellow bee replaces the "M" in the top bar, the first-run screens and the browser tab.
- **New ideas have no code.** A new card no longer gets an `I12`-style id; existing ids stay.

## 0.1.37 · 16 Nov 2026

**The skill you install once no longer goes out of date, and the app stays open in Codex.**

- **A small starter.** The `maybee` skill in your home folder is now three files: if the project has Maybee installed it hands over to the skill inside the package, otherwise it installs it. What changes with each version lives in the package, so updating Maybee updates how your assistant behaves.
- **To switch:** install `maybee.zip` of this release once in place of your old `maybee` skill.
- **The app stays open when Codex ends a command.** The app, the Skool window and the bridge are started in a session of their own, on Linux and macOS; `start.sh` does it whenever an assistant runs it. Checked on Linux with a real Codex; not tried on macOS yet.
- **`maybee.sh` asks to run outside Codex's sandbox** instead of saying "not running" about an app that runs.

## 0.1.36 · 16 Nov 2026

**Your assistant starts, stops and restarts Maybee with one command, and the bridge stays on.**

- **`/maybee` does the right thing.** Alone, it makes everything run: the app and the bridge, leaving alone what already runs. With a word it is `start`, `stop`, `restart`, `listen`, `status` or `update`.
- **The bridge starts with the app.** The install is not finished until the app says "Listening" with the assistant's name.
- **Codex: no time limit, and no silent failure.** The bridge keeps answering until you stop it. Started inside Codex's sandbox, it now stops at the start and says to run it outside the sandbox, instead of saying "Listening" and failing every request.
- **One bridge at a time** answers for a workspace.

## 0.1.35 · 16 Nov 2026

**Run and Ask AI reach the agent you already work with: Codex or Claude Code.**

- **Codex works.** The bridge started from your Codex session sends each request from the board back to that same session, with its context. The status tray names the agent and the session.
- **A session open in a window.** Codex does not let a second process take over a session that is open, so Maybee answers from a fork of it and keeps going in that fork. The answers show in the app, not in your open window.
- **A run that fails closes the request.** If the agent ends without an answer or takes too long, the request gets a reply that says why.
- **Claude works as before.** The start command now carries `--agent claude`.

## 0.1.34 · 16 Nov 2026

**Change the project by asking, from About.**

- **A chat on About.** Write what should change in the voice, audience, offer, brand, decisions or knowledge notes, and the assistant edits the right file straight away and logs it in the changelog. When the file is unclear, it asks.
- **Updated marks.** A file the chat changed shows "Updated" with the time in the Memory list, until you open it or a day passes. Each workspace has its own chat and marks.
- **Files are links.** A file named in a reply opens with a click.

## 0.1.33 · 15 Nov 2026

**Put an image, a video, a page or a PDF inside a note, and see it right there.**

- **`![](file)` shows the file.** A line with only `![alt](path)` or `![[file]]` is drawn in the note: an image or a video, or a `.html` or `.pdf` page in a frame (also an `https` address ending in `.html` or `.pdf`). It works in the reading view and in the editor.
- **Editing shows the text.** A double click turns editing on and the Markdown is shown as typed; Esc or leaving the note draws the files again.
- **A framed page is sandboxed.** Its scripts run, but without access to Maybee's own address, so it cannot call the app or read your notes.
- **Only alone on a line.** In the editor, an image inside a sentence stays as text.

## 0.1.32 · 14 Nov 2026

**Sort the Maybe column the way you want, see why a card was set aside, and always know which column a note is in.**

- **Maybe sorts six ways.** The Sort menu on the column orders it by priority, easy first, value for members, value for business, most recent, or unrated first. A sort writes the new order into each card's `rank`, so it replaces an order set by hand; Undo puts the earlier order back for ten seconds. Cards without all four reactions go last, except in "Unrated first".
- **A card in Not shows why.** The reason is the `why_not` line of the note, set in Settings, or else the first paragraph under a "Why not" heading.
- **A stage badge beside the title** of every card you open, with the color of its column.
- **Replies in the note chat are Markdown**: lists, bold and code are drawn. Raw HTML is never shown.
- **Fixes.** `knowledge/offer/product.md` in the template had a description with an unquoted colon, which is not valid YAML (existing workspaces: quote the `description` line). Links with `%2C` are no longer reported as broken.

## 0.1.31 · 14 Nov 2026

**Follow the links in your notes, edit tables right where they are, and find your files faster in About.**

- **Notes open for reading.** A click on a link (a `[text](file.md)`, a web address or a `[[wiki link]]`) follows it. A double click turns editing on, with a yellow border; Esc, or leaving the note, turns it off. Ctrl or Cmd plus click follows a link while you edit. Saving stays automatic, and a note you have not typed in is never rewritten.
- **Tables are drawn as tables.** While you edit, the cells are typed in directly: Tab, Shift+Tab and Enter move between them, Tab in the last cell adds a row, "+ Row" and "+ Column" sit under the table, and "Edit as text" shows the Markdown to remove a row or column or set an alignment. The first edit rewrites the table as `| a | b |` with `| --- |` under the header; the content and the alignment stay.
- **About lists your files in a clearer way.** Each group starts with its README, shown as Overview. A file is called by the `title` in its frontmatter, else by its file name read as words ("design-elements" is "Design elements", "PROJECT" is "Project"); a dated decision loses its date from the name. Under the name you see only the `description` from the frontmatter (or the date of a decision), never the text of the note.
- **A ring shows what is still empty.** Beside the name of an audience, offer or brand file: dashed when nothing is filled in, an arc while it is partly filled, solid with a tick when complete. Hover it for "3 of 16 filled".
- **New workspaces start with descriptions.** The template's knowledge files carry a `description`, and `decisions/README.md` describes a decision with `title`, `description`, `date` and `status` in its frontmatter. Files you already have are not changed: add `description:` to their frontmatter to see it in About.

## 0.1.30 · 14 Nov 2026

**Move any note, find your audience, offer and brand in About, and let updates tidy your files on their own.**

- **A note with no frontmatter can be moved.** Maybee adds the frontmatter itself (the title from the file name) the first time the card is moved, ranked or its settings change. It used to refuse. A `---` at the top that never closes counts as part of the text, not as frontmatter.
- **About lists your foundations.** Audience, Offer, Brand and Decisions each have their own group, and each file shows how much of it is filled in. A section counts as filled when it has an answer: the prompt and the empty bullet do not count.
- **The Owner group is gone from About.** Your owner notes are still in `owner/`; About now lists the project, audience, offer, brand, decisions and the loose knowledge notes.
- **Older workspaces can get the new files.** When a workspace lacks the template's `knowledge/` and `decisions/` files, About offers to add them. `migrate.js fill` does the same. It only adds what is missing and never changes a file that exists.
- **Updates move your files by themselves.** The app applies pending migrations to every workspace when it starts after an update: files are moved, links to them and inside them are fixed, and a copy of what changed stays in the workspace's `.maybee/backup/`. If an old and a new copy of a file both exist and differ, both are left alone and `migrate.js plan` still lists the migration. This corrects 0.1.29, whose notes said a migration waited for your OK.

## 0.1.29 · 14 Nov 2026

**Add a workspace in one click, start it from a template that knows your audience, offer and brand, and let updates move files without breaking your links.**

- **Add workspace.** The workspace menu has an "Add workspace" item. It creates a new workspace from a blank template and opens its Workspace board. No assistant is involved.
- **A template with real structure.** `knowledge/audience/` (one file per person you serve), `knowledge/offer/` (a ladder of products, and one file per product), `knowledge/brand/` (your voice and the design elements that make you recognizable) and `decisions/` (one file per decision). Every section is a question and an empty line: a section you leave empty is skipped, never guessed.
- **One Maybee skill.** The board rules are now a guide inside the `maybee` skill, next to a guide for writing from the files above and one for moving files. The separate `maybee-board` skill is removed.
- **Updates can move your files, only with your OK.** Your assistant runs `migrate.js plan`, tells you what would move, and applies it after your yes. It fixes the links that point to the moved files and the links inside them, keeps a copy in `.maybee/backup/`, and never runs the same migration twice. If both the old and the new file exist and differ, it leaves them alone. This version proposes `owner/voice.md` to `knowledge/brand/voice.md` and `knowledge/decisions.md` to `decisions/register.md`.
- **Links stay correct.** To move or rename a file your assistant uses `migrate.js move`, and `migrate.js links` lists every link that points at nothing.
- **Routines have no stage.** A routine is a card with `type: routine`; its column comes from that, not from `stage` (`stage: not` still hides it).
- **The app reloads itself** when Maybee restarts, and the workspace menu shows why when a workspace cannot be opened.

## 0.1.28 · 14 Nov 2026

**Move cards with a click, ask your assistant the right thing for each column, and let it use a browser.**

- **Buttons to move a card.** Maybe has "Do it next", Next has "Start now", Now has "Mark done". Going back a column or to Not is one hover away ("Back to Next", "Not now"), on the board and at the top of the note. Done has "Reopen". Marking a card done from the board also records the date.
- **The assistant's quick asks follow the column.** In Maybe it helps you explore the idea, in Next it looks for gaps, refines and checks if the card is ready, and in Now it works on it.

- **Routines look like every other card.** The same card, with the Run button, the Not today and Remove menu, the auto-run gear and the run order. The empty box under the routines is gone.
- **Cleaner cards.** The Ask AI button and the "You + agent" label are gone from the cards, and so is the ticket number (T1, I3). It is still in the note's file. Ask your assistant through the chat inside the note.
- **About shows the goal and the focus.** The goal from the project brief and the focus from STATE sit right under the title.
- **Your assistant can use Maybee's browser, in a window of its own.** For a task that needs a web page, it opens a new window in Maybee's browser (the one with your Skool login), never the app window. It only works after the membership check, it never reads your passwords or cookies, and anything that goes out, like posting or messaging, waits for your OK.

**Maybee opens on the Workspace board.**

- **The window opens on the board.** When Maybee opens its window, including when your assistant opens it at the start of a session, it lands on the Workspace board of the active workspace. About is still in the menu, it is just not the first screen anymore.

## 0.1.26 · 14 Nov 2026

**A badge for what changed, workspaces that show up on their own, and titles you can edit.**

- **New workspaces appear without a reload.** A workspace created from outside the page, by your assistant for example, shows in the menu at the top within seconds, and right away when you open the menu or come back to the window.
- **The card title is the note's first heading.** Edit the `# title` inside the note and the card on the board follows. Before, a card without a `title` line showed the file name. The `title` line and then the file name are still the fallback.

## 0.1.25 · 13 Nov 2026

**Write an idea straight into Maybe, and arrange your cards by hand.**

- **A field at the bottom of the Maybe column.** Type an idea and press Enter: it becomes a card at the top of the column, and the field stays ready for the next one. Nothing is sent to your assistant.
- **Drag cards to put them in your own order.** In Maybe, Next and Now, drop a card above or below another one. The order is kept in the cards, so it travels with your files. A card you move to another column lands at the top of it.
- **Sort by priority, once.** A link under the Maybe title puts the column in priority order. After that you can still drag cards wherever you want.
- **One button less at the top.** The Story / Everything switch moved into Activity as "Project files", next to the filters. Files like STATE, README, owner/ and knowledge/ are always in the Vault, where you can open and edit them.
- **Maybee's own files stay out of sight.** Nothing inside the `.maybee` folder shows up in Activity, the Vault or search anymore, and notes can no longer open it. Before, a few of its files could appear in Activity with Project files on.
- **One dot for the assistant.** The Claude and Live buttons became a single dot at the top. Green: connected and a session is listening. Grey: connected, but nobody is listening. Red: the app is unreachable. Click it for the requests and the details.
- **Same page width everywhere.** Activity now has the width of the Vault and Overview.
- **Maybee opens on the Workspace.** It no longer reopens on the page you left. Workspace is now first in the menu, and Overview, now called About, is last.
- **About is now the project's cover.** It shows the project's name and summary, how many cards are in Now, Next, Maybe and done this week, shortcuts to the Workspace, Activity and Vault, and the project's own files (the brief, the state, the owner and knowledge notes). The long lists of focus, Now and decisions are gone from it: they live in the Workspace and in `knowledge/decisions.md`. "Waiting for you" stays when something needs a decision.
- **A clearer "Not listening".** The assistant's bridge lasts about 30 minutes, then your Claude session has to start it again. Maybee now says when the last one stopped and what to write in Claude to start it, and the session is told to restart it by itself when it ends.

## 0.1.24 · 13 Nov 2026

**Ask AI is now a chat at the bottom of every note, and notes are always ready to edit.**

- **A chat that floats at the bottom of the note.** Write to your assistant about the note you are reading, from anywhere on the page. The conversation about that note, with the answers, opens above it. Enter sends, Esc hides it. The button at the top of the note is gone.
- **Notes open ready to edit.** No Edit button: click and type. Your changes are saved a moment after you stop typing, and when you switch notes or tabs. Titles keep their yellow underline, and the links at the top of a note (Dashboard, Start here) stay out of the way while you edit.
- **Changes made elsewhere are taken in quietly.** If your assistant or the Settings tab changes the note while it is open, the change appears without losing what you typed. Only when the text itself changed on both sides, Maybee asks which version to keep.
- **The changelog stays readable.** Typing saves often, so a note adds one changelog line every 10 minutes at most.
- **Your assistant gets requests without gaps.** The bridge to your assistant can stay on and hand over each request as it comes, instead of stopping after every one.

## 0.1.23 · 13 Nov 2026

**Fixed: checking your membership no longer opens a pile of windows.**

If Maybee was restarted while its window stayed open, the next membership check could not connect to that window, opened another one, and every new window started the check again. Maybee now reuses the window that is already open, a check that fails to start is not repeated by itself, and a failed attempt waits a few seconds before it can be retried.

## 0.1.22 · 13 Nov 2026

**Ask AI and Run reach your assistant without gaps.**

- The bridge between the board and your assistant can now stay alive and hand over each request as it arrives, instead of stopping after every one. Before, a request sent while your assistant was busy waited with "Not listening" until the bridge was started again.
- Your assistant is now told how to start the bridge so that requests wake it up. A bridge started the wrong way showed "listening" but never delivered anything.

## 0.1.21 · 13 Nov 2026

**Fixed: updating Maybee no longer gets blocked by its own protection.**

Maybee's protection stopped your assistant from running the update, even after you said yes. Updates now go through, and the protection still stops any other change to Maybee's files.

## 0.1.20 · 13 Nov 2026

**Fixed: "Ask AI" and "Run" now work without asking your assistant to start Maybee first.**

Opening a session with your assistant in this workspace now starts Maybee if it is not running and connects the board's Run and Ask AI buttons to that session. Before, the buttons only saved the request ("Not listening") until the assistant had been asked to start Maybee.

## 0.1.19 · 13 Nov 2026

**A cleaner tab, and a short answer when asked to change Maybee.**

- The browser tab now says "Maybee", without another project's name.
- If you ask your assistant to change Maybee itself, it now declines in a sentence and keeps working on your files, without going into details.

## 0.1.18 · 13 Nov 2026

**Fixed: the starter cards now show up on a new install.**

On a fresh install, Maybee opened your project folder instead of the Default workspace, so the board looked empty even though the starter cards were there. It now opens Default and remembers it.

## 0.1.17 · 13 Nov 2026

**Maybee's program files are protected, and every update tells you what changed.**

- When Maybee updates, a card appears in Now with what is new. If you skip a few versions, you get a card for each of the latest ones. Read it and move it to Done.
- Maybee's program files are now read-only, and your assistant is told not to change them, even if you ask it to improve Maybee. Maybee gets better through updates. Your own notes, ideas and files are not affected.
- What Maybee saves while it runs now lives in `.maybee/data/`, next to the program and not inside it. Your existing data is carried over.
- Maybee is now delivered pre-built: a smaller download that sets up with a single dependency instead of dozens.
- Your assistant now has a short guide to how cards work (`maybee-board`), so the cards it creates or changes show up on the board the way they should.
- The workspace list shows Default and the workspaces you created, nothing else.
- Fixed stray folder paths that showed up inside sentences in the starter cards.

## 0.1.16 · 13 Nov 2026

Nothing changes inside the app. We fixed how Maybee is published, so the latest version can always be downloaded from one stable address.

## 0.1.15 · 13 Nov 2026

**Maybee checks your membership with its own browser, and opens in its own window.**

- When you open Maybee, it checks that you are a member of the How I community. It does this on its own now, with a Chromium-based browser it launches (Chrome, Edge, Brave or Chromium, whichever you have installed), without needing your assistant to browse for you.
- If you are already signed in to Skool, the check finishes by itself. A Skool window opens only when you need to log in.
- Maybee keeps only what it needs to confirm access: your user, the community, your role and when you were approved. It never stores cookies or passwords.
- The app now opens in that same browser as a window of its own, instead of a tab in your default browser.
- The demo mode that skipped the check is gone. There is no way around the membership check.

This release was tested on Linux only.

## 0.1.0 to 0.1.2 · 11 Nov 2026

**The first release.** Maybee, its board and its installer, ready to add to a Claude or Codex workspace.
