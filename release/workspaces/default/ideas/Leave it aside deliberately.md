# What Not means

This card explains the **Not** column. Every idea in Maybe gets one decision, and Not is where the **no** goes. It is not a forgotten backlog: it is what you deliberately chose not to pursue, together with the reason, so you never have to decide it twice. It stays hidden until you bring the pointer to the left edge of the board, or drag a card there. Not is also the only place where a card can be deleted.

**First step:** move an idea here only when you can say why it is not worth attention now.
