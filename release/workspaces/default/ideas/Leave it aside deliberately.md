# What Not means

This card explains the **Not** column. Not is not a forgotten backlog: it is where you put something you deliberately chose not to pursue, together with the reason. It stays hidden until you bring the pointer to the right edge of the board.

**First step:** move an idea here only when you can say why it is not worth attention now.
