# What is Maybee

**Maybee turns your ideas into things you actually finish.**

You keep your ideas in one place. Maybee, together with an assistant, helps you pick the next step and do the work, one small step at a time.

## The three parts

- **A board.** Your ideas live on it as cards, and they move from left to right as they become real: from a thought, to a plan, to what you are doing now, to done. You see all of it at a glance.
- **An assistant.** Maybee works with an AI assistant, Claude or Codex, that you connect from your own computer. You talk to it in the chat bar at the bottom of every card: ask about a card, or tell it what to do with it. It reads your project first, so it never starts from zero.
- **Your files.** Everything is a plain file in a folder of yours, with no hidden database, so you can open any of it outside Maybee. The **Brain** shows all of it, and you can ask it questions. The app and your files stay on your computer, and anything that goes out into the world (posting, sending, publishing) waits for your approval.

![The board, opened on Now](maybee-board.webp)

## What it is not

It is not a chat window that forgets you, and it is not a to-do list that only stores things. It is a board and an assistant working from the same files, so what you decide stays decided.

You can think of it as a second brain that does not stop at remembering: it helps you finish.

## A small round

1. Write an idea in Maybe.
2. Ask Maybee for a few next steps, and pick one.
3. Start it, with the assistant helping.
4. See what Maybee made in the **Inbox**, approve it, and mark the card done. It stays as history.

That is the whole loop. Everything else in Maybee is there to make that loop easier.
