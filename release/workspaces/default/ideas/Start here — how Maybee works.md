# How Maybee works

This is the tour: the method, the board, the pages, and where to talk to Maybee. If you have not read *What is Maybee* yet, start with that one.

Everything you see here is yours: your notes, ideas, decisions and routines are plain files in your workspace, and Maybee shows them as a board you work on with an assistant.

## It all starts with Maybe

Maybee starts with *Maybe*, and so does every idea. That is the method.

Most tools ask you to commit the moment you write something down: a task, a due date, a promise. Maybee does not. An idea first goes to [**Maybe**](maybee:board/maybe), where it can wait at no cost. You can look at it again, explore it with Maybee, and learn what it is worth, without owing anything to it.

From Maybe, every idea gets one honest decision:

- **No.** It goes to **Not**, with the reason written down, so you never have to decide it twice.
- **Yes.** It becomes a plan in **Next**, moves to **Now** when you work on it, and ends in **Done**.
- **Not yet.** It stays in Maybe. That is a decision too.

Because nothing has to be promised to get in, you can write down every idea. Because every idea passes through Maybe, what reaches Now is something you really chose. Your first idea is already there: if you answered Maybee's questions, it is your goal, and it explains the column. If you skipped them, *Capture an idea* does.

## Your board

The columns follow that path, from left to right. Maybee always opens on [Now](maybee:board/now). Click a column's name below and the board opens it while this card stays open, so you can read and look at the same time.

![The board, opened on Now](maybee-board.webp)

- [**Not**](maybee:board/not): ideas you said no to, each with its reason. It hides at the left edge until you point at it or drag a card there (look at the board before you click this link and you will not see it). It is also the only place where a card can be deleted. *What Not means* explains it.
- [**Maybe**](maybee:board/maybe): where every idea starts, with no promise attached.
- [**Next**](maybee:board/next): ideas that became a plan, ready and waiting for Now to have room. This tour is here.
- [**Now**](maybee:board/now): the few things worth your attention today. *What is Maybee* is the one you find here: read it, then move it to Done.
- [**Done**](maybee:board/done): finished work, kept as history so you can see how far you have come. It starts with *What Done means*.
- **Routine**: work that comes back by itself, like a daily check-in. It hides at the right edge, the mirror of Not, and slides out when you point at it. A yellow number there tells you how many routines are ready to run today. You will find *How routines work* there.

This is Not appearing when you point at the left edge of the board:

And this is Routine, on the other side, with its yellow number:

**Capture from anywhere.** Under all the columns there is a bar: *Write an idea and press Enter. It goes to Maybe.* The new card shows up in Maybe, highlighted, and the cursor stays there for the next one. Drop a file on the bar and the new idea carries it.

## One action per column

Every card offers the one step its column needs, in yellow on its chat bar:

- **Explore this idea** in Maybe: Maybee gives a rough idea a clear title and description, and looks at what it could become.
- **Get it ready to start** in Next: Maybee makes the plan concrete enough to begin.
- **Do the next step** in Now: Maybee does the next piece of work with you.
- **What comes next?** in Done: Maybee suggests what could follow.

Start typing and the same spot turns into **Send**, so you can always ask for something else instead.

## Where things are

The top bar has three places:

- [**Workspace**](maybee:go/workspace): the board.
- [**Inbox**](maybee:go/inbox): what Maybee made for you, like mail. **Needs you** holds what waits for your OK; **For you** holds what Maybee made, one line per card, bold until you open it. It has its own chat, so you can answer or approve without leaving it.
- [**Brain**](maybee:go/brain): all your files. Your project, its state and its dashboard sit on top, the folders below. Type to search; press Enter to ask a question, and Maybee answers from your files, with links. Tell it what changed ("I now work with schools too") and it updates your goal, audience or voice in the right file. You can also move, rename and delete files here, and every link to them is fixed.

## Inside a card

When you open a card, it has three tabs:

- **Content:** the note itself: what it means, why it exists, what done looks like, and the next step. When Maybee makes something for the card (a draft, an image, a page), it shows here under **Work**, newest first.
- **Settings:** the controls Maybee uses to place it on the board: type, column, owner, priority, schedule.
- **Changes:** the history of the file: what changed and when.

The button next to ✕ lets a long card fill the window; press it again to put it back beside the board.

![A card open beside the board, with its tabs and the chat bar](maybee-card.webp)

**Try it now:** click **Content**, **Settings** and **Changes** on this card, then ask Maybee what one of the fields means.

## Your first card

When you finish here, open [your first card](Turn%20the%20work%20into%20a%20post.md) in Maybe. It is the goal you gave Maybee, and it explains that column too (if you skipped the questions, it is *Capture an idea*). Like every card, it is a plain file in your workspace, in the `ideas` folder, so you can also edit it outside Maybee.

**Done when:** you know what each column is for, where the Inbox and the Brain are, and you have chosen the first thing to move forward.

## Talk to Maybee

At the bottom of every card there is a chat bar: *Ask Maybee about this note, or tell Maybee what to do…*. Ask about the card you are reading, or tell Maybee to do something with it. Click the paperclip, drop a file, or paste a screenshot to give Maybee something to read first. With a card open, the chat follows your mouse: over the board you see the board's bar, over the card you see the card's chat.

The bee beside the bar tells you who is there: **green** when an assistant is connected and listening, **yellow and swaying** while it works on your request, **grey** when no assistant is connected yet. Anything that goes outside your workspace (posting, sending, publishing) still waits for your OK.

**First step:** type in the chat bar below, "What should I do first in this workspace?"
