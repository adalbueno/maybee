# How Maybee works

This is the tour: the board, the pages, and where to talk to Maybee. If you have not read *What is Maybee* yet, start with that one.

Everything you see here is yours: your notes, ideas, decisions and routines live in your workspace, and Maybee shows them as a board you can work on with an assistant.

## Your board

Maybee always opens on [Now](maybee:board/now). Click a column's name below and the board opens it while this card stays open, so you can read and look at the same time.

![The board, opened on Now](maybee-board.webp)

- [**Not**](maybee:board/not): look at the board before you click this link and you will not see it. It hides at the left edge until you point at it or drag a card. This is where an idea goes when you decide *no*, with the reason written down, so you never have to decide it twice. *What Not means* explains it.
- [**Maybe**](maybee:board/maybe): the heart of an idea. Everything starts here, with no promise attached: a thought you want to keep, look at again, and maybe never do. If you answered Maybee's questions, your goal is the first idea here, and it explains the column too. If you skipped them, *Capture an idea* does.
- [**Next**](maybee:board/next): the idea has become a plan. These are the steps that are ready and waiting for Now to have room. You will find this tour here, next in line, and *Choose a next step*.
- [**Now**](maybee:board/now): the few things worth your attention today, and the column Maybee always opens on. *What is Maybee* is the one you find here: read it, then move it to Done.
- [**Routine**](maybee:board/routine): work that comes back by itself, with a schedule, an automation and a Run button. It is not a one-off task. You will find *How routines and settings work*, the daily How I post.
- [**Done**](maybee:board/done): finished work, kept as history so you can see how far you have come. It starts with a small example, *What Done means*.

This is Not appearing when you point at the left edge of the board:

![Not, revealed at the left edge of the board](maybee-not.webp)

## About: tell Maybee who you are

The [About](maybee:go/about) page, in the top bar, is where Maybee keeps what everything else depends on: your goal and focus, what is waiting for your decision, and the project's own files (who it is for, what you offer, how you sound). It has its own chat: say in plain words what changed ("I now work with schools too") and Maybee updates the right file, keeps its language and format, and logs the change. Come back to About whenever the project changes.

## Where things are

- [**Workspace**](maybee:go/workspace): the board.
- [**Activity**](maybee:go/activity): every change and new file, newest first.
- [**Vault**](maybee:go/vault): all your files in one place.
- [**About**](maybee:go/about): your project, and the chat that changes it.
- Community: [**How I on Skool**](https://www.skool.com/how-i-9301)

## Working with Maybee

Ask the agent to capture an idea, write a draft, explain a note, or decide what to do next. It should read this workspace first and keep the files updated. Anything that goes outside the workspace — posting, sending or publishing — still waits for your explicit approval.

When you open a card, the viewer has three tabs:

- **Content:** the note itself — what it means, why it exists, what done looks like, and the next step.
- **Settings:** the note's controls and metadata — its type, stage, owner, priority, schedule, and other fields Maybee uses to place it on the board.
- **Changes:** the history of the file — what changed, when it changed, and which part of the project it came from.

Every card opens like this: its text, the three tabs, and the chat bar at the bottom.

![A card open beside the board, with its tabs and the chat bar](maybee-card.webp)

**Try it now:** open this card, click **Content**, **Settings**, and **Changes**, then ask Maybee what one of the fields means.

**Done when:** you know where the files live, what Now/Next/Maybe mean, and you have chosen the first thing to move forward.

## Your first card

When you finish here, open [your first card](Turn%20the%20work%20into%20a%20post.md) in Maybe. It is the goal you gave Maybee, and it explains that column too (if you skipped the questions, it is *Capture an idea*). Like every card, it is a plain file in your workspace, in the `ideas` folder, so you can also edit it outside Maybee.

## Talk to Maybee

At the bottom of every card there is a chat bar: *Ask Maybee about this note, or tell Maybee what to do…*. Ask about the card you are reading, or tell Maybee to do something with it, and the answer shows up in the chat. The bee beside the bar tells you who is there: **green** when an assistant session is connected and listening, **yellow and swaying** while it works on your request, **grey** when no session is connected yet.

**First step:** type in the chat bar below, "What should I do first in this workspace?"
