# How routines work

This card explains the **Routine** column. A routine is work that comes back, not a one-off task. It stays after today's run and returns when it is due.

The Routine column hides at the right edge of the board, the mirror of Not. Point at the right edge and it slides out. A yellow number there tells you how many routines are ready to run today.

- **Run** starts it now. Your assistant does the work and answers in the card's chat.
- **‹ Not today** skips it for today; it comes back tomorrow.
- The **Settings** tab is where you choose how often it comes back, whether it starts by itself while an assistant is connected, and where the cards it makes land (Maybe, Next, Now, or nowhere).

To make your own routine, drag any card onto the Routine column, or tell Maybee in a chat, "make this a routine every Monday". To stop one, send it to Not: it becomes an ordinary card again.

## What this routine does

Every day, when you press Run:

1. Read the cards in Now and Next, and what waits in the Inbox under **Needs you**.
2. Suggest the one thing most worth doing today, and why, in two or three sentences.
3. Point out anything that has been sitting in Now for too long.

It only reads and suggests. It never moves a card or sends anything on its own.

**Done when:** today's suggestion is in the chat and `last_done` is updated here.

**First step:** press **Run**, or ask Maybee to change this routine into one that fits your work.
