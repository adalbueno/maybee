# Choose a next step

This card explains the **Next** column. Next is the short queue of tasks you have chosen to do, but are not doing yet. It is not a second Maybe: the work here is ready to move forward.

Ask Maybee for a few possible next steps when you are unsure. Pick the one that makes the most useful progress with the least friction, then keep it here until Now has room. Maybee can turn an idea into a task when you decide to act on it.

**Done when:** one task is clear enough to move to Now and has a concrete first action.

**First step:** ask Maybee, "Give me three possible next steps from this workspace," choose one, and move it to Now when you are ready to work on it.
